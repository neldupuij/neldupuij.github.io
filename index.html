§3 — Spillovers

Research

Working paper · October 2026 · with Prof. Duc Thi Luu

Volatility Spillovers and Connectedness Across Equities of European Financial Institutions

How do volatility shocks travel through Europe's banking system? Using the Diebold–Yilmaz framework (rolling VAR + generalized FEVD), we measure total, directional and net spillovers across listed banks, insurers and financial firms, benchmark them against DCC-GARCH and MST correlation networks, and zoom in on country-level connectedness.

  • Persistently high system-wide connectedness, with a sharp jump during COVID-19.
  • Post-COVID connectedness stays above pre-2019 levels — a structural rewiring of long-run linkages.
  • Institutions switch roles between emitter and receiver across frequency bands.
  • A core–periphery hierarchy between national banking systems.

Presented at FMND 2026 — 10th International Workshop on Financial Markets and Nonlinear Dynamics, Paris · also served as paper discussant
